About GRADIENTX

The best AI shouldn't be a corporate perk.

The most capable AI today serves enterprises and experts. GRADIENTX exists to point it the other way: an agent system for ordinary people's everyday lives — starting with money, the part nobody teaches you.

Who we are

Researchers, not bankers

GRADIENTX was started by researchers, not bankers — with roots at the University of British Columbia and Dalhousie, building from Vancouver.

We started with money because the gap is personal: students, newcomers, and first-paycheque professionals are handed a complex financial system and no manual. Our answer is FinTorch — a system of agents that teaches, watches out for you, and lets you practice safely.

We're early, and we say so. What we bring is research-grade rigor: privacy treated as architecture, guidance built to cite verified sources, and claims we can stand behind.

What comes next

Vision

Money is where we start, not where we stop. The same agent system — private by design, grounded in verified sources, built to explain itself — is how we plan to take on the rest of everyday life.

How we work

Rules we've set for ourselves

Honest about stage

We're pre-launch, with no customers yet — and this page will never pretend otherwise. Status labels on our site say what's built, what's in development, and what's still vision.

Privacy is a constraint, not a feature

No bank login required, strong encryption, an anonymous on-device mode. These are architecture decisions being made before launch — not patches promised after.

Built with the people it serves

FinTorch is being shaped with the people it's for — through conversations with students, newcomers, and first-paycheque professionals — the beginners it exists for, not a boardroom's idea of them.
