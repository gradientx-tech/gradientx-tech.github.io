Privacy Policy隐私政策

What PetAI keeps on your Mac, what it keeps online, and why.PetAI 哪些信息留在你的 Mac 上，哪些放在线上，以及为什么。

Last updated November 7, 2026最后更新：2026 年 11 月 7 日

Stays on your Mac只留在你的 Mac 上

  • Your chats with your pet, what you teach it, and what it remembers (its memories and diary).
  • Your voice: speech is turned into words on your Mac, and the sound is never uploaded.
  • Your pet's model and every version it learns in class.
  • The content check: it runs on your Mac. The words it checks never leave it.
  • 你和宠物的聊天、你教它的内容、它记得的事（记忆和日记）。
  • 你的声音：语音在你的 Mac 上转成文字，声音本身不会上传。
  • 宠物的模型，以及它上课学出来的每一个版本。
  • 内容安全检查：在你的 Mac 上运行，被检查的文字不会离开你的电脑。

Kept online, and why放在线上的信息，以及原因

  • Your account: your email address, a scrambled form of your password (a salted hash; we can't read your password), when you confirmed you're 18 or older, which terms you accepted, and whether your email is verified. Needed to sign you in.
  • Your pets: each pet's name and time zone, its coins and how it earned or spent them, what it owns (food, furniture, toys, brain size), and its daily check-in streak. Kept online so coins and items can't be faked.
  • Pet School: its exam and class results (scores, certificates, and the checks after a class), and its answers to the daily quiz. Its answers to exams are sent to be graded. Kept so grading is fair and its records stay with it.
  • Warnings: how many, which kind, and when. Never the words. If you appeal, what you write in the appeal.
  • Weather: where you are (the location you share, or else the city of your Mac's time zone), rounded to about 10 km, is sent to get the weather. We don't store it in our database; our server's request logs may record it for up to 30 days.
  • 你的账号：邮箱地址；密码的加密形式（加盐哈希，我们看不到你的密码）；你确认年满 18 岁的时间；你同意的是哪一版条款；邮箱是否已验证。这些用于让你登录。
  • 你的宠物：每只宠物的名字和时区、它的宠物币以及每笔币的来去、它拥有的东西（食物、家具、玩具、大脑大小）、每日签到的连续天数。放在线上是为了让宠物币和物品不能被伪造。
  • 宠物学校：考试和上课的结果（分数、证书、上完课后的检查），以及每日小测的答案。考试的答案会发到线上判分。保存这些是为了判分公平，也让它的记录一直跟着它。
  • 警告：次数、类别和时间，从不包括原文。如果你申诉，会保存你写的申诉内容。
  • 天气：你所在的位置（你共享的位置；没共享的话，就用你 Mac 时区对应的城市）会被模糊到约 10 公里，用来查天气。我们不把它存进数据库；服务器的请求日志可能会记录它，最多保留 30 天。

Who else gets it还有谁会接触到这些信息

  • Google Cloud runs our server and database (in the United States).
  • Resend sends the emails with your sign-in codes, so it gets your email address.
  • Hugging Face: the first time, PetAI downloads its models from Hugging Face, which sees your IP address as any website would.
  • Environment and Climate Change Canada and the US National Weather Service get the rounded location, from our server, not from you.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • Google Cloud 运行我们的服务器和数据库（位于美国）。
  • Resend 负责发送带验证码的邮件，所以会拿到你的邮箱地址。
  • Hugging Face：第一次打开时，PetAI 会从 Hugging Face 下载模型。和访问任何网站一样，它会看到你的 IP 地址。
  • 加拿大环境与气候变化部和美国国家气象局会收到模糊后的位置。这些请求由我们的服务器发出，不是从你的电脑直接发出。
  • 我们不出售你的信息，不放广告，也不使用任何追踪或统计分析工具。

How long we keep it保存多久

  • Online data is kept while your account exists. Write to gradientx.tech@gmail.com from your account's email to have your account and its online data deleted; we'll do it within 30 days.
  • A sealed account that isn't restored within 30 days has its pets and their online data deleted.
  • What's on your Mac is yours to keep or delete: it's in ~/Library/Application Support/PetAI.
  • 账号存在期间，线上数据会一直保存。想删除账号和线上数据，请用账号邮箱写信到 gradientx.tech@gmail.com，我们会在 30 天内删除。
  • 被封存的账号如果 30 天内没有恢复，它的宠物和线上数据会被删除。
  • 你 Mac 上的数据由你自己决定保留还是删除，位置在 ~/Library/Application Support/PetAI。

Keeping it safe安全

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Your sign-in is kept in the macOS Keychain, and we store only a hash of it. Sign-in codes expire after 15 minutes. Repeated wrong passwords lock the account for a while.

密码只以加盐哈希的形式保存。登录凭证保存在 macOS 钥匙串里，我们只保存它的哈希。验证码 15 分钟后失效。连续多次输错密码，账号会被暂时锁定。

Your rights你的权利

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we keep online about you: write to gradientx.tech@gmail.com.

你可以要求查看、更正或删除我们在线上保存的你的信息，请写信到 gradientx.tech@gmail.com。

Age年龄

PetAI is only for people 18 or older. If we learn an account belongs to someone younger, we close it and delete its data.

PetAI 只供年满 18 岁的人使用。如果发现账号属于未满 18 岁的人，我们会关闭账号并删除它的数据。

Changes and contact修改与联系

If we change this policy, the app will tell you. Questions: gradientx.tech@gmail.com. See also the Terms of Use.

如果我们修改这份政策，App 会告诉你。有疑问请联系：gradientx.tech@gmail.com。另见使用条款。
