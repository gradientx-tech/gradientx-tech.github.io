Install安装步骤

  1. Download the installer above and open it.
  2. Drag PetAI into the Applications folder.
  3. Open PetAI from Applications. macOS says it can't check the app: click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to PetAI, and confirm. (On macOS 14 you can instead right-click PetAI → Open.)
  4. Sign up with your email (you must be 18 or over) and enter the 6-digit code we email you.
  5. The first time, PetAI downloads its models (about 4–5.5 GB, see below). Leave it open until it's done; chat starts once the content check is ready.
  1. 下载上面的安装包，双击打开。
  2. 把 PetAI 拖进「应用程序」文件夹。
  3. 在「应用程序」里打开 PetAI，系统会提示无法验证这个 App：点「完成」，然后打开「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」，往下找到 PetAI，点「仍要打开」并确认。（macOS 14 上也可以直接右键点 PetAI → 打开。）
  4. 用邮箱注册（需要年满 18 岁），输入我们发到你邮箱的 6 位验证码。
  5. 第一次打开时，PetAI 会下载它的模型（约 4–5.5 GB，见下方）。下载完之前请保持打开；内容安全检查准备好之后就能聊天了。

What your Mac needs电脑要求

Chip芯片 Apple chip (M1 or later). Intel Macs aren't supported.Apple 芯片（M1 或更新）。不支持 Intel 芯片的 Mac。
System系统macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. macOS 13 and older aren't supported.macOS 14（Sonoma）或更新。不支持 macOS 13 及更早的系统。
Memory内存 16 GB or more recommended. 16 GB uses the Qwen3-1.7B brain; from 24 GB, Qwen3-4B. 8 GB hasn't been tested and is likely too tight while it's in class.建议 16 GB 或以上。16 GB 用 Qwen3-1.7B 作为宠物的大脑，24 GB 起用 Qwen3-4B。8 GB 还没测试过，上课训练时很可能不够用。
Disk space硬盘空间 About 6 GB free: the app (about 0.6 GB), its brain (1–2.3 GB), the content check (1.5 GB), and its ears for voice (1.6 GB). 约 6 GB 可用空间：App 本身（约 0.6 GB）、宠物大脑（1–2.3 GB）、内容安全检查（1.5 GB）、听懂说话的语音模型（1.6 GB）。
Internet网络 Needed to sign in, for the first downloads, and for Pet School, coins and the shop. Chatting at home works offline. 登录、第一次下载、宠物学校、金币和商店需要联网；在家聊天不联网也可以。

Tested on Apple-chip Macs with macOS 15 and macOS 26.

已在 macOS 15 和 macOS 26 的 Apple 芯片 Mac 上测试过。

Your privacy隐私

  • Your pet runs on your Mac. Your chats, what you teach it and what it remembers stay on your computer.
  • Voice is understood on your Mac too; your voice is never uploaded. You can uninstall voice in My skills.
  • 宠物运行在你的 Mac 上。你们的聊天、你教它的东西、它记得的事都留在你的电脑里。
  • 语音识别也在你的 Mac 上进行，声音不会上传。不想要语音的话，可以在「我的本领」里卸载。

Content rules使用规则

Sexual content, sexual content involving anyone under 18, and violence, hate or crime aren't allowed, in chat or in what you teach it. Each one is a warning. Three warnings seal the account (sexual content involving a minor seals it at once); you can appeal. If an account isn't restored within 30 days of being sealed, its pets are deleted.

不允许色情内容、涉及未满 18 岁者的性内容，以及暴力、仇恨或违法内容，聊天和教学都一样。每次违反记一次警告；满 3 次账号会被封存（涉及未成年人的性内容会立即封存），可以申诉。封存后 30 天内没有恢复，宠物会被删除。
