Install安装步骤

  1. Download the installer above and open it.
  2. Drag Modlet into the Applications folder. (Had PetAI, its old name? It updates itself to Modlet: no need to download it again.)
  3. Open Modlet from Applications. macOS says it can't check the app: click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Modlet, and confirm. (On macOS 14 you can instead right-click Modlet → Open.)
  4. Sign up with your email (you must be 18 or over) and enter the 6-digit code we email you.
  5. The first time, Modlet downloads its models (about 4–5.5 GB, see below). Leave it open until it's done; chat starts once the content check is ready.
  1. 下载上面的安装包，双击打开。
  2. 把 Modlet 拖进「应用程序」文件夹。（装过它的旧名字 PetAI？它会自动更新成 Modlet，不用重新下载。）
  3. 在「应用程序」里打开 Modlet，系统会提示无法验证这个 App：点「完成」，然后打开「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」，往下找到 Modlet，点「仍要打开」并确认。（macOS 14 上也可以直接右键点 Modlet → 打开。）
  4. 用邮箱注册（需要年满 18 岁），输入我们发到你邮箱的 6 位验证码。
  5. 第一次打开时，Modlet 会下载它的模型（约 4–5.5 GB，见下方）。下载完之前请保持打开；内容安全检查准备好之后就能聊天了。
  1. Download the installer above and open it.
  2. The installer isn't signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen warns you: click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Keep the internet on: the installer downloads PyTorch (about 2.6 GB) and checks it. This takes a while; leave it running.
  4. Open Modlet from the Start menu. Sign up with your email (you must be 18 or over) and enter the 6-digit code we email you.
  5. When you adopt a pet you choose its model (0.6B or 1.7B); it downloads the first time.
  6. To update, install the new version over the old one. Don't uninstall first: your pets stay, and PyTorch isn't downloaded again.
  1. 下载上面的安装包，双击打开。
  2. 安装程序还没有签名，Windows 会弹出 SmartScreen 警告：点「更多信息」，再点「仍要运行」。
  3. 安装时请保持联网：安装程序会下载约 2.6 GB 的 PyTorch 并核对校验和，需要一段时间，请不要关掉。
  4. 从开始菜单打开 Modlet，用邮箱注册（需要年满 18 岁），输入我们发到你邮箱的 6 位验证码。
  5. 领养宠物时选模型大小（0.6B 或 1.7B），第一次会下载模型。
  6. 更新时直接安装新版本覆盖旧版，不要先卸载：宠物都会保留，PyTorch 也不用重新下载。

What your Mac needs电脑要求

Chip芯片 Apple chip (M1 or later). Intel Macs aren't supported.Apple 芯片（M1 或更新）。不支持 Intel 芯片的 Mac。
System系统macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. macOS 13 and older aren't supported.macOS 14（Sonoma）或更新。不支持 macOS 13 及更早的系统。

Disk space硬盘空间 About 6 GB free: the app (about 0.6 GB), its brain (1–2.3 GB), the content check (1.5 GB), and its ears for voice (1.6 GB). 约 6 GB 可用空间：App 本身（约 0.6 GB）、宠物大脑（1–2.3 GB）、内容安全检查（1.5 GB）、听懂说话的语音模型（1.6 GB）。
Internet网络 Needed to sign in, for the first downloads, and for Pet School, coins and the shop. Chatting at home works offline. 登录、第一次下载、宠物学校、金币和商店需要联网；在家聊天不联网也可以。

Your privacy隐私

  • Your pet runs on your computer. Your chats, what you teach it and what it remembers stay on your computer.
  • Voice is understood on your computer too; your voice is never uploaded. You can uninstall voice in My skills.
  • 宠物运行在你的电脑上。你们的聊天、你教它的东西、它记得的事都留在你的电脑里。
  • 语音识别也在你的电脑上进行，声音不会上传。不想要语音的话，可以在「我的本领」里卸载。

Content rules使用规则

Sexual content, sexual content involving anyone under 18, and violence, hate or crime aren't allowed, in chat or in what you teach it. Each one is a warning. Three warnings seal the account (sexual content involving a minor seals it at once); you can appeal. If an account isn't restored within 30 days of being sealed, its pets are deleted.

不允许色情内容、涉及未满 18 岁者的性内容，以及暴力、仇恨或违法内容，聊天和教学都一样。每次违反记一次警告；满 3 次账号会被封存（涉及未成年人的性内容会立即封存），可以申诉。封存后 30 天内没有恢复，宠物会被删除。
